# Edits to the copy

This directory is the fork's `apps/web` at the commit in `UPSTREAM`, as far as the thread bundle's entry (`../src/`) reaches it; `pnpm vendor:merge` keeps it so (`pult/decisions`, `thread-view-source`). What a thread tab needs from the app around the view lives in files of ours: `../src/` mounts upstream's components, and `../stubs.ts` replaces whole upstream modules. Editing a copied file is the exception, made when neither can do it; each edited file is listed here, one bullet with its reason (`` - `src/a.ts`: why ``), and the merge carries it through upstream's changes. `pnpm vendor:check` fails on a copied file that differs from upstream at `UPSTREAM` and is not listed, and on a listed file that does not differ.

- `src/components/chat/ComposerFooterControls.tsx`: `ComposerControlsSlot`, a context the app around the view may fill with an element, and `ComposerControlsSlotItem`, which portals an item there; with a slot, the context window meter renders in it. Pult's shell puts the slot's items in its thread pane's composer bar (`../shell/ThreadView.tsx`); without a slot the composer is upstream's.
- `src/components/chat/ChatComposer.tsx`: with a `ComposerControlsSlot`, the footer's settings (`ComposerControlsBar`: model, traits, mode) render in the slot, in their expanded form while the composer rests, and so do its attach and send buttons, leaving the composer its message alone; the slot, the app's element, sits at the end of the composer's form while it is mounted (`../shell/ComposerBar.tsx`'s `ComposerBarHost`), so its buttons submit the form; the notices, the sync row and the task progress render in the slot too, as entries of ours (`../shell/ComposerBar.tsx`), not above the composer.
- `src/components/ChatView.tsx`: with a `ComposerControlsSlot`, the draft's environment and branch strip renders in the slot, the resting model strip does not mount, and the composer's shell leaves no seam for either; and a timeline following its end stays there as the composer's reserved room grows, since the shell's composer is opaque.
